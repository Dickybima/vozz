01 Game Features

Build Your Rig

Assemble rigs from dozens of GPUs, PSUs, risers, and frames. Every part has its own hashrate, power draw, and thermal profile.

Overclock & Tune

Dial in core clock, memory clock, and power limit. Squeeze out every last hash — but push too hard and your cards will overheat and crash.

A Living Blockchain

Difficulty adjusts in real time across the entire player network. Find blocks, claim rewards, and climb the global leaderboard.

Mining Pools

Join a pool or start one with friends. Split the rewards, plan your strategy, and dominate the network with your guild.

03 FAQ

Does VOZZ mine real crypto?

No. VOZZ is a simulation game. All mining happens inside the game and never uses your device's GPU to mine real cryptocurrency.

What do I get for joining the waitlist?

Early access to the closed beta, an exclusive starter GPU, and a 500 VZ (in-game currency) bonus at launch.

Which platforms will VOZZ be on?

VOZZ launches first in the browser (desktop & mobile), with native apps to follow.

Is it free to play?

Yes, VOZZ is free to play. Optional cosmetics will be available, but there is no pay-to-win.
