03 FAQ

Which blockchain does VOZZ use?

VOZZ is built on Robinhood Chain, an Ethereum Layer 2 built on Arbitrum (chain ID 4663, gas paid in ETH). Any EVM wallet such as MetaMask works — your Robinhood Chain address is the same 0x… address you use on Ethereum.

Does VOZZ mine real crypto?

No. The mining itself is a simulation: it happens inside the game and never uses your device's GPU to mine cryptocurrency. Robinhood Chain is used for player wallets and rewards.

What do I get for joining the waitlist?

Early access to the closed beta, an exclusive starter GPU, and a 500 VOZZ (in-game currency) bonus linked to the Robinhood Chain address you submit.

Which platforms will VOZZ be on?

VOZZ launches first in the browser (desktop & mobile), with native apps to follow.

What is the Mining Pass?

To start mining you need a VOZZ Mining Pass — an NFT on Robinhood Chain that costs 0.001 ETH (plus gas), one per wallet. Connect your wallet in the game, mint the pass, and your rig unlocks. The pass is an access key to the game, not an investment, and it doesn't promise any return.
